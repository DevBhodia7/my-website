About

Hardware that has to work in front of the person who needs it.

I'm a final-year engineering student at the University of Wollongong in Dubai. My work has ranged from CubeSat avionics at SpacePoint to antenna design in UOWD's research lab, and to founding Vantage Vision, where I lead the hardware, embedded and AI development of Halo, a wearable for blind and visually impaired users.

What ties it together is a habit of checking the design against the requirement before anything gets built, testing it with the people it's for, and keeping the records straight so the next engineer can follow what changed.
