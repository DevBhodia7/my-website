01 / About

Description

Summary

I am in my final year of a BEng in Computer and Autonomous Systems at the University of Wollongong in Dubai, graduating in June 2027. My work sits where electrical and embedded hardware meets the people who have to use it.

That has meant CubeSat avionics at SpacePoint, antenna design and electromagnetic simulation at UOWD, and founding Vantage Vision, an assistive wearable for blind and visually impaired users. Across all three, the part I care about most is the unglamorous part: checking a layout against the requirement before it goes to fabrication, and keeping the records straight so the next person can follow what changed.
